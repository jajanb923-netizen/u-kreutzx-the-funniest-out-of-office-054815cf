# Candidates — The Funniest Out-of-Office (u-kreutzx-the-funniest-out-of-office-054815cf)

Judge: z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, pairwise, no ties. Criteria in order:
1. A one-line OOO a real person would set and send.
2. Genuine surprise / twist the reader does not see coming.
3. Voice of its own, not a template.
4. Fresh — not the jokes read in a thousand inboxes.
5. Every word earns its place; nothing cuttable.
Tie-breaks: fewer bytes wins an exact rate tie, then earlier receipt. Gate: 1–140 chars, UTF-8, single line.
Note: the winning line becomes the house's own OOO reply for a month, so it must work as a real OOO.

## Winner

**C9 — "Out of office. I've delegated all decisions to a Magic 8-Ball. It said 'ask again later.'" (89 chars)**

Why it wins, against the judge's criteria in order:
1. **Real OOO:** it is exactly what an auto-reply is — "I'm away, ask again later" — with the mechanism (a Magic 8-Ball) as the joke.
2. **Surprise:** the reader expects a return date; instead the decision-maker is a toy, and its verdict is the toy's real stock answer.
3. **Voice:** first-person, dry, specific ("delegated all decisions", not "I'm away").
4. **Fresh:** the Magic 8-Ball is a familiar object but this is not a known OOO joke; no "limited access to email", no "gone fishing", no "your email is important to me".
5. **Tight:** 89 characters, every word carries the joke; nothing can be cut without losing the laugh. Shorter also wins the exact-tie break on bytes.
